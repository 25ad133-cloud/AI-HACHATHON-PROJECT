Academic Regulations and Credits:
The undergraduate program runs on a choice-based credit system (CBCS) spanning 8 semesters.
To successfully complete the degree, a student must accumulate a minimum of 160 credits as specified in the curriculum.
Grades are awarded on a 10-point scale: O (Outstanding - 10), A+ (Excellent - 9), A (Very Good - 8), B+ (Good - 7), B (Above Average - 6), C (Satisfactory - 5), and U (Re-appear - 0).
The Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) is calculated at the end of each semester as the weighted average of the grade points obtained in all courses.
A minimum CGPA of 5.0 is required to obtain the degree certificate.
Students can register for a maximum of 28 credits per semester, including audit courses and value-added courses.
Course registration must be completed within the first week of the start of the semester.
