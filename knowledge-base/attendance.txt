Attendance Policy and Regulations:
Students must maintain a minimum of 75% attendance in all courses to be eligible to appear for the end-semester examinations.
If a student has attendance between 65% and 74% due to medical reasons, they may be permitted to write the exams upon submission of a valid medical certificate and payment of a condonation fee.
Students with attendance below 65% are not eligible to write the exams under any circumstances and must repeat the semester in the subsequent academic year.
Lack of attendance will result in immediate withholding of the hall ticket.
Attendance for laboratory sessions, practical exams, and internal assessments is also subject to the same 75% rule.
