Examination Rules and Guidelines:
Students must arrive at the examination hall at least 15 minutes before the scheduled start time of the exam.
No student will be allowed to enter the exam hall after 30 minutes from the commencement of the exam.
It is mandatory for students to carry their valid physical college Hall Ticket and Identity Card to every exam session.
No electronic gadgets, including smartwatches, mobile phones, or programmable calculators, are permitted inside the examination hall.
Any form of malpractice, copying, or possession of unauthorized materials will lead to immediate disqualification and disciplinary action by the controller of examinations.
The minimum passing mark for any course in the end-semester examinations is 50%.
A student who fails to secure passing marks can apply for revaluation within 7 days of result declaration or register for the supplementary exams.
