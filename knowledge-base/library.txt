Library Hours and Rules:
The college central library is open from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM on all working days (Monday to Saturday).
On Sundays, the library operates on reduced hours, from 9:00 AM to 4:00 PM.
The library remains closed on public holidays and official college holidays.
Students can borrow up to 4 books at a time for a maximum duration of 14 days.
A fine of 5 Rupees per day is charged for late return of books.
Strict silence must be maintained inside the library premises.
No food or drinks are allowed inside the reading halls.
Using mobile phones for calls is strictly prohibited inside the library.
