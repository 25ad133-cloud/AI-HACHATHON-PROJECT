Hostel Rules and Guidelines:
The curfew timing for all hostellers is 9:00 PM. All students must return to the hostel premises and register their attendance by this time.
No student is allowed to leave the hostel after curfew without written permission from the Warden.
Cooking in the rooms using electric stoves, heaters, or any other appliances is strictly prohibited.
Visitors are allowed inside the hostel reception only between 4:00 PM and 6:30 PM on weekdays, and 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM on Sundays.
No visitor, including parents, is permitted to stay overnight in the student rooms without prior written approval from the Warden.
Possession or consumption of alcohol, tobacco, or drugs inside the hostel premises is a severe offense and will result in immediate expulsion from the hostel.
Silence hours are observed from 10:00 PM to 6:00 AM daily to facilitate quiet study and sleep.
Students are responsible for the cleanliness and maintenance of their allotted rooms and common areas.
